About us

Two musicians and one very large house.

We are Marena and Max Whitcher. We play music, and for a long time we recorded it in other people's rooms, on other people's schedules.

In 2024 we bought a villa at the edge of St-Ursanne that had stood empty for eleven years. It took two winters, most of our savings, and a lot of help from the neighbours. The plaster is original. The wiring is not.

Now it is a studio and it is also where we live. We cook lunch. We keep out of the way when you want to be left alone. If a session runs late, nobody has to drive anywhere.

Marena & Max WhitcherSt-Ursanne

The Space

Three rooms, a garden, and good light in all of them.

Nothing here is soundproofed into a box. The rooms have windows, wooden floors, and the acoustics of a house that was built to be lived in.

The Live Room

Parquet, 3.6 metre ceilings and three windows onto the park. It takes a drum kit, a string quartet, or twelve people singing at once.

The Control Room

A small analogue front end running into a modern rig. One sofa, one window. Most control rooms do not have a window.

The Salon

The upright piano, the record player, and the chairs nobody gets out of. Most of the writing happens in here, usually after midnight.

The Park & the Forest

Two hectares of private park that runs straight into the woods. The Doubs is ten minutes downhill. Go there when the take is not working.

Overnight & Residency

Nobody has to drive home.

Rooms
Four doubles, six beds. Linen and towels are there when you arrive. The house is quiet after ten.
Kitchen
One big kitchen and a table that seats twelve. Breakfast is included. We will cook dinner if you would rather keep working.
Residency
One to four weeks with the house to yourself and unlimited studio time. We take a handful each year. Write to us about the work.
Getting here
Train to Saint-Ursanne, then eight minutes on foot. Basel is an hour away, Zürich two. We will collect you and the gear from the station.
