About us

We're Marena and Max Whitcher — a musician couple who fell for a big old house at the edge of a medieval town.

For years we recorded on the road, in other people's rooms, always at somebody else's clock. So we built the place we kept wishing existed: unhurried, personal, full of light and instruments and far too many plants.

Whitchcraft Studio is our home as much as our workplace. We cook, we listen, we get out of the way. When you come here you are our guest first and a client second.

Marena & Max

02

The Space

Four rooms and a garden that all do something different — and a house that lets you move between them without breaking the thread.

The Live Room

Parquet, high ceilings and three tall windows. Warm and lively, with enough air for strings, drums or a choir of friends.

The Control Room

An analogue front end into a modern rig. Comfortable enough to sit in for twelve hours, honest enough that you don't have to.

The Salon

An upright piano, a record player and the good chairs. Where most of the writing actually happens, usually after dinner.

The Park & Forest

A private park that runs straight into the woods. The best place to go when the take isn't working — the Doubs river is a ten-minute walk.

03

Overnight & Residency

Stay a night, or stay a fortnight. The work goes differently when you don't have to drive home.

Rooms
Four bedrooms, sleeping up to six. Linen, towels and a very quiet night included.
Kitchen
A big shared kitchen built for long dinners. Breakfast is on us; we'll cook the rest if you'd rather keep working.
Residency
One to four weeks, self-directed, studio time included. A handful of slots each year — tell us what you're making.
Getting here
Two hours from Zürich, one from Basel. Train to Saint-Ursanne, then a short walk uphill — we'll come and get you and the gear.
